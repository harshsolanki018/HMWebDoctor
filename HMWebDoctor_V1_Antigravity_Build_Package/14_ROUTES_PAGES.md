# Routes and Pages

## Required public routes
/
  Landing page

/how-it-works
  Explain diagnosis process

/scan
  URL submission and progress

/report/:publicId
  Shareable/public report

/about
  Product/company information

/contact
  Professional contact form

/privacy
  Privacy policy

/terms
  Terms of service

/404
  Not found

## Optional V1 routes
/dashboard
  Scan history

/dashboard/scans
  Scan history list

/dashboard/websites/:id
  Website scan history

/login
/register
Only include these if authentication is deliberately enabled in V1.

## Professional naming
Use user-facing names such as:
- Website Health
- Diagnosis
- Findings
- Treatment
- Scan History
- Report
- How It Works
- About
- Contact
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service

Avoid childish labels such as "Doctor Mode" or "Emergency Room".
